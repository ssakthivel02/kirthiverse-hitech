## Change control

Re-review is required when the responsible process says so, including after material changes such as:

- different participant age band;
- new school/organisation context;
- materially different data collection/storage/sharing;
- introduction of cloud child identity/account features;
- introduction of live AI interactions;
- material metrics/outcome-study change;
- new external service/provider;
- significant incident or safeguarding/privacy finding;
- material product change affecting the reviewed risk boundary.
