## 1. What is KirthiVerse?

KirthiVerse is a learning prototype being evaluated in a small controlled pilot. The current pilot focuses on bounded learning activities such as mathematics diagnostic questions, lessons, Quick Skills practice and local progress signals.

The controlled-pilot build is **not being presented as a production school platform**. It does not provide a verified cloud child account or a live AI tutor.

## 2. Why is this pilot being proposed?

The pilot is intended to observe whether the prototype can be used reliably in a small supervised setting and to collect limited, descriptive evidence about use of the learning activities.

The pilot is **not designed, by itself, to prove learning improvement, retention, comparative effectiveness or school-wide outcomes**.

## 3. What would a participant do?

Subject to the final approved pilot plan, a participant may:

- use a non-identifying learner alias on the pilot device;
- complete selected learning activities;
- take a maths diagnostic or Quick Skills activity;
- work through lessons and answer checks;
- use bounded hints/worked guidance only if the approved pilot protocol allows it;
- stop using the pilot at any time in accordance with the final withdrawal process.

The final approved version of this information must state the expected session frequency, approximate session length, pilot duration and any supervision arrangements.

## 4. What information does the current controlled-pilot build store?

The current evidence design is local-first. Depending on the activities used, the browser may store local evidence such as:

- a non-identifying learner alias;
- learning progress/status;
- diagnostic results;
- Quick Skills activity records;
- answer-check activity;
- conservative active-learning time on specified learning routes;
- local pilot start/end and readiness snapshots;
- local evidence exports generated by an authorised adult/operator.

The controlled-pilot evidence process is designed **not** to require a real child name, participant email, school identifier or cloud child account in the KirthiVerse evidence files.

The final reviewed information must accurately reflect the actual build and operating process used for the pilot.

## 5. Where is information stored?

The current controlled-pilot measurements are stored in the browser on the pilot device unless an authorised adult deliberately exports an evidence JSON file for the approved pilot process.

The final approved information must explain:

- who controls the device;
- where approved exports are stored;
- who can access them;
- how long they are kept;
- how deletion/withdrawal requests are handled;
- whether any other systems are introduced outside KirthiVerse.

## 6. What is not verified by the app?

The current build does not independently verify:

- the identity of a parent/guardian;
- the legal validity of consent/permission;
- school approval;
- safeguarding approval;
- research ethics approval.

Any required permission/consent/assent process must therefore be handled through the responsible external process approved for the pilot.

## 7. Possible inconveniences or risks to review

The external reviewer should assess and finalise wording for reasonably foreseeable risks, including:

- frustration or fatigue from learning activities;
- inappropriate interpretation of prototype scores as formal educational assessment;
- accidental disclosure if a shared browser/device is not properly controlled;
- local data loss if browser storage is cleared;
- privacy risk if operators enter real identifying information instead of approved aliases;
- inappropriate continuation if a safeguarding/privacy incident occurs;
- overclaiming pilot activity as evidence of learning outcomes.

The final participant information should include only risks/mitigations approved as accurate for the actual pilot.

## 8. Benefits

Do not promise an educational benefit.

A cautious final description may state that participants may experience prototype learning activities and may help the project understand usability and controlled-pilot operation. Any stronger benefit statement requires separate evidence and reviewer approval.

## 9. Voluntary participation and stopping

The final approved process should state clearly:

- participation is voluntary where applicable;
- a participant/adult can ask to stop according to the approved process;
- stopping does not require the participant to continue using the prototype;
- how already-exported pilot evidence will be handled when withdrawal/deletion is requested, subject to the approved retention/process rules;
- who to contact.
