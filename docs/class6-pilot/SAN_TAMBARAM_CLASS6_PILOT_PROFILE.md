# KirthiVerse HI-TECH — CBSE Class 6 Pilot Profile

## Pilot intent

This pilot uses SAN Academy Tambaram as the school-specific validation overlay while keeping the underlying KirthiVerse curriculum model reusable for CBSE schools generally.

## School-aligned Class 6 subject set

Based on SAN Academy Tambaram's published Primary & Secondary curriculum, the Class 6 pilot must cover:

1. English — Language and Literature
2. Second Language — Tamil or Hindi
3. Third Language — Tamil or Hindi for Class VI
4. Mathematics
5. Science
6. Social Science
7. Life Skills & Aptitude
8. Computer Science
9. Health and Physical Education
10. Work Education
11. Art Education

Language choices are school/student specific and must remain explicit in the learner profile. KirthiVerse must not assume one universal second/third-language combination.

## Assessment overlay

For Classes VI–VIII, SAN Academy publishes a two-term CBSE-aligned assessment model with Half Yearly and Annual examinations. Scholastic assessment is 80 marks through the term examination plus 20 marks of internal assessment: Periodic Test 5, Portfolio 5, Multiple Assessment 5, and Subject Enrichment Activity 5. Work Education, Art Education, and Health & Physical Education are graded as non-scholastic activities.

KirthiVerse may use this as a pilot assessment overlay, but must not claim to replace the school's official assessment or report card.

## Kiki teaching contract

Every completed pilot chapter must support the following learner path:

Class 6 → Subject → Chapter → Kiki teaching → worked examples / visual explanation → guided practice → quiz → remediation → mastery evidence.

Canonical guide wording: "I’m Kiki, your KirthiVerse guide."

Adaptive explanation wording: "Why Kiki picked this for you".

Kiki must remain deterministic/reviewed for the pilot. No unrestricted child chat, microphone capture, external learner prompts, or child-facing cloud AI interaction is enabled by this profile.

## Completion definition

A subject is not labelled `COMPLETE_CBSE_CLASS6` until every applicable chapter in the approved source baseline has:

- source/provenance mapping;
- chapter and competency mapping;
- a Kiki-teachable lesson;
- examples or demonstrations appropriate to the subject;
- guided practice;
- assessment coverage;
- remediation path;
- mastery evidence;
- accessibility review;
- rights/source-status review;
- validation against the school pilot profile where applicable.

## Donor policy

Historical `ssakthivel02/kirthiverse` content, old exports, Library files, Gemini outputs, and Claude outputs are read-only donor material. They may be reconciled into this HI-TECH repository only after confirming the equivalent capability/content is absent and the donor item is source-safe, structurally compatible, and not duplicated.

Do not bulk-copy legacy content.

## Pilot sequencing

1. Foundation manifest and provenance ledger.
2. Mathematics chapter map and first complete Kiki teaching slice.
3. Science chapter map and first complete Kiki teaching slice.
4. Social Science.
5. English.
6. Explicit Tamil/Hindi second/third-language tracks after source and school-book reconciliation.
7. Computer Science.
8. Life Skills & Aptitude.
9. Work Education.
10. Art Education.
11. Health and Physical Education.
12. Whole-Class-6 coverage audit and pilot evidence report.

Do not expand to other classes until Class 6 demonstrates end-to-end usefulness and quality.
